Prepositions of Time

We use the prepositions in, on or at to say when something happens.

The original classroom lesson
Open the PDF below to review its examples and quick reference.

IN / ON / AT · Quick reference

Examples below come from the classroom PDF.

IN

  • Months: in January / in April
  • Seasons: in spring / in winter
  • Years: in 1984 / in 2015
  • Centuries / longer periods: in the 20th century / in the 1990s / in the past / in the holidays
  • Parts of day: in the morning / in the evening

ON

  • Days: on Monday / on Tuesdays
  • Dates: on November 22nd
  • Day + part: on Tuesday afternoon / on Saturday mornings
  • Special days: on my birthday / on New Year's Eve

AT

  • Clock times: at 7.30 a.m. / at 5 o'clock
  • Festivals: at Christmas / at Easter
  • Expressions: at night / at the weekend
