Imperative Practice

Practise commands, instructions and advice for safer social media use.

A. Complete the sentences

Use each word once: like, comment, follow, share, check.

like · comment · follow · share · check

1. ______ your favourite posts every day.

2. ______ your messages when you wake up.

3. ______ on your friends' posts to support them.

4. ______ positive accounts that make you happy.

5. ______ the post if you find it funny.

B. Put the words in the correct order

Write a complete command. Remember: the imperative starts with the verb.

1. check / every / your / day / messages

2. share / your / post / with / friends

3. make / your / account / private

4. only / post / good / photos

5. like / your / don't / strangers' / posts

C. Good advice or bad advice?

Choose whether each command is good or bad advice. If it is bad, rewrite it as a positive suggestion for online safety.

1. Post your location every time you go out.

2. Keep your password simple and easy to guess.

3. Follow positive and inspiring accounts.

4. Don't reply to messages from people you don't know.

5. Share your personal information on social media.
